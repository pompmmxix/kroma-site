How this works

  • You don't need to create an account. A dedicated bot account posts your message to the public feedback list on our behalf, so you stay anonymous.
  • The message becomes public. The feedback list is open to anyone — that's the trade-off for not running a private server. Don't put anything in the description you wouldn't say in public.
  • No attachments. Don't paste photos, screenshots, or files. Describe in words instead — "the cultural-info screen for Banarasi lists Bengal as the origin, but Banarasi is from Varanasi in Uttar Pradesh" is more useful than a screenshot anyway.
  • No identifying information. Don't put your name, email, phone number, or address in the description. We don't need them and they'd be public.

That's not something we can help with from the Kroma side. Kroma connects you to whichever AI model you've configured (Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, or a local model). We don't train, run, or improve any of them — they belong to their respective providers.

Two things usually help:

  • Edit the fields by hand. The AI's reading is a starting point, not the final word — every field is editable. You have the last say.
  • Use a more capable model. If you're on a free tier (e.g. Gemini Flash), a paid tier of the same provider often reads photos far better. That usually means an API key with credits — costs vary by provider, and what you pay goes to them, not to us.

If your concern is actually about something Kroma controls — a UI bug, a missing tradition, a factual error on our side — pick one of the other three options instead.
