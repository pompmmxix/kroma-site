Fill your wardrobe from your order emails

The Kroma order extension is a free add-on for Google Chrome. It reads the order confirmations in your Gmail, lists the clothes you bought, and makes one file you import into the Kroma wardrobe app. Years of orders become a wardrobe in a few minutes, with pictures, sizes, prices and purchase dates.

Your emails are read inside your own browser. They are not sent to us, to an AI service, or to anyone else. Kroma has no server and no accounts. The full detail is in the extension's privacy policy.

The extension is in testing and is not yet on the Chrome Web Store. To try it, email hello@kroma.fit.

How it works

  1. Connect Gmail. Open the extension, choose how far back to look, and sign in with Google. Access is read-only: the extension cannot send, delete or change any email.
  2. It finds your orders. It searches for order, receipt and dispatch emails, reads the items in each one, and joins the several emails a shop sends about the same order. Orders you returned or cancelled are marked.
  3. You choose what to keep. Clothing is listed first, each item with its picture, size and price. Tick what you still own. Things that aren't clothing start unticked.
  4. Download the file and import it. Move the file to your phone, then in Kroma open Settings, Backup & Restore, Import an order file. Nothing is added to your wardrobe until you confirm it there.

What it reads, and why

Read-only access to Gmail

Used to find and read your order emails: sender, subject, date and content. Gmail offers no narrower permission that covers only receipts.

Only to build your file

What it reads is used for one thing: listing the clothes you bought. It is not used for advertising, never sold, and never used to train AI.

Processed in your browser

The reading is done by code in the extension, on your computer. No email content leaves it, and nothing is kept when you close the tab.

Pictures from the shops

With your permission, it fetches each item's picture from the shop's website, or from the Internet Archive if the shop has removed it. Those requests carry the product's link or name only.

The extension's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See the privacy policy for the full statement.

What to expect

  • It gets you most of the way, not all of it. Shops lay out their emails in many ways. Orders it can't read are listed with a link to open each one, so you can add them in Kroma from a screenshot instead.
  • Older orders may have no picture. When a shop has deleted a product's picture and no archive kept it, the item imports without one. Replace it with your own photo in Kroma.
  • Gmail only, for now. Outlook and Hotmail are planned.
  • Google Chrome on a computer. The extension does not run on phones.
  • Product names in English work best. In other languages, items are still found, but you may need to set the garment type in Kroma.

Removing access

Click Disconnect Gmail in the extension, remove it at myaccount.google.com/connections, or remove the extension from Chrome. The extension stores nothing, so there is nothing else to delete.
