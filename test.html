Try kroma before it launches

kroma reads your colouring from a selfie in five colour systems, then scores the clothes you own, and the ones you're thinking of buying, against your palette. It's free, there's no account, and your wardrobe stays on your phone.

The beta is open now on Android and iPhone. We're looking for people who'll use it for a couple of weeks and tell us honestly what works and what doesn't.

How to join

Pick whichever is easier:

  1. On Instagram: send a DM that says TEST to @kroma.fit.app.
  2. By email: write to hello@kroma.fit.

Either way, tell us two things:

  • Your phone: Android or iPhone.
  • The email for your invite. On Android this must be the Google account you use on the Play Store. On iPhone any email works.

What happens next

Android: we add your email to the test and send you a link to join on Google Play. Open it, tap to join, then install kroma from the Play Store. Please stay in the test for at least 14 days: updates and reinstalls are fine, but leaving the test means Google stops counting you.

iPhone: you'll get an email invite from Apple's TestFlight (check spam if it's not there). Install the free TestFlight app, then tap View in TestFlight in the email to install kroma.

We're one person, so invites are sent by hand, usually within a day.

What we'd love you to try

  1. Scan your colours from a selfie.
  2. Add five things from your wardrobe.
  3. Plan one outfit.

Then tell us what confused you and what would make you open kroma every day. Reply to our DM or email, or use the feedback page.

Your details

We use your email only to invite you to the test and send test updates. It's shared with Google or Apple solely to send your invite, never sold or used for marketing, and deleted within 30 days of the test ending, or sooner if you ask. Reply STOP or email hello@kroma.fit to leave. The details are in our privacy policy.
