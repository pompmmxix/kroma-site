Your wardrobe, private by design.

Catalogue your clothes. See what you actually wear. Get outfit suggestions that fit your weather, your body, and your palette. Your data never leaves your phone unless you choose to send it.

What Kroma does

Catalogue everything

Photograph each garment. AI auto-tags the type, colour, pattern, weave, and fabric where visible. 120+ garment types across world traditions.

Log what you wear

Snap an outfit photo and AI identifies which items from your wardrobe you're wearing. See real cost-per-wear, not guesses.

Weather-aware outfits

Manual chips by default (zero network), or opt in to Open-Meteo once a morning using a city you type. Personal comfort calibration means your "warm" isn't someone else's.

Colour & body analysis

12-season Western, Ritu (South Asian), Shikisai (Japanese), Korean Tonal, and Wu Xing (Chinese) analysis systems — plus Kibbe and proportion-based body analysis.

Learn what you own

Tap a pattern, colour, or fabric on any garment to see its origin, transmission, and cultural context — with attribution honest to where traditions actually come from.

Private by architecture

No account. No server. No tracking. Bring your own AI API key so photos go direct to the provider you choose. Or run it fully offline with Ollama.

How the privacy piece actually works

Kroma does not run a backend. Your wardrobe data lives on your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising IDs, no crash reporters. The few places data can leave your device — the AI provider you chose, the weather service you opted in to, a product page you shared to the app — are listed exhaustively in the privacy policy.

If something in the app's attribution of a cultural tradition looks wrong to you, or if we're missing a tradition that should be there, tap the "Something off?" or "Know a tradition we're missing?" links on any info modal, or email hello@kroma.fit directly. We'd rather hear from you and get it right.
