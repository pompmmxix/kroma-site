details:
- heading: Best hair colours for Warm Autumn
  body: |
    Your natural hair is often auburn, copper, golden brown or warm dark blonde. Rich, warm colour suits you best.

    - **Flattering:** copper, auburn, chestnut, golden brown, warm caramel and honey highlights.
    - **Harder to wear:** ash blonde, platinum, blue-black and cool, smoky browns. They drain the warmth from your skin.
- heading: Jewellery and metals
  body: |
    Yellow gold is your metal, along with bronze, copper and brass. Hammered, textured and antique finishes look especially good. Silver tends to look cold on you.

    For stones and materials, choose warm and earthy ones: amber, tiger's eye, citrine, topaz, carnelian, coral, turquoise, wood and tortoiseshell.
- heading: Denim, prints and fabrics
  body: |
    - **Denim:** mid to dark warm-blue washes, plus rust, olive, camel and chocolate cords and chinos. Grey or icy-blue washes look cold on you.
    - **Prints:** earthy and spicy: paisley, autumn florals, leopard, tortoiseshell, plaids and ikat in rust, mustard, olive and teal.
    - **Fabrics:** warm, textured fabrics: suede, tweed, corduroy, chunky knits, leather and raw linen.
- heading: Colours Warm Autumn should avoid
  body: |
    The least flattering colours are cool, icy or very bright: the opposite of warm and rich.

    - **Black** and **optic white**
    - **Icy pastels** such as icy pink, icy blue and lavender
    - **Fuchsia**, **magenta** and **blue-reds**
    - **Cool greys** and **silver-grey**

    Wear them away from your face, or bring a warm colour like camel, rust or olive up near your face.
- heading: Warm Autumn vs Warm Spring
  body: |
    Both are warm first, so both suit golden colours and gold metals. The difference is depth and clarity.

    | | Warm Autumn | Warm Spring |
    |---|---|---|
    | Feel | Rich, muted, earthy | Clear, fresh, sunny |
    | Best oranges | Rust, pumpkin, terracotta | Tangerine, apricot |
    | Best greens | Olive, moss, deep teal | Grass green, warm turquoise |
    | Best yellows | Mustard, marigold | Golden yellow, buttercup |
    | Neutrals | Chocolate, camel, olive | Light camel, warm ivory, golden tan |

    A quick test: hold rust and bright tangerine under your face in daylight. If rust looks natural and tangerine looks loud, you're more likely Warm Autumn.
- heading: Warm Autumn vs Soft Autumn and Deep Autumn
  body: |
    All three Autumns suit warm, earthy colours. **Soft Autumn** is the most muted: salmon rather than orange, khaki rather than mustard. **Deep Autumn** is darkest: espresso, burgundy and forest green. Warm Autumn sits between them, with the most golden, spicy palette of the three.
faq:
- q: Is True Autumn the same as Warm Autumn?
  a: >-
    Yes. Different colour analysis systems use different names for the same season. True Autumn and Warm Autumn both describe the warmest Autumn type.
- q: Can Warm Autumn wear black?
  a: >-
    It's one of the harder colours for Warm Autumn, because it's cool and stark. Chocolate brown, deep olive and navy look richer. If you love black, wear it below the waist or add gold jewellery and a warm colour near your face.
- q: What white should Warm Autumn wear?
  a: >-
    Cream, ivory or warm oatmeal. Optic white looks stark against golden skin.
- q: What pinks suit Warm Autumn?
  a: >-
    Warm pinks with a lot of orange or brown in them: coral, salmon, terracotta pink and peach. Icy pink and fuchsia are the hardest.
- q: What are the best neutrals for Warm Autumn?
  a: >-
    Navy, charcoal, beige, cream, camel, chocolate and olive. Build a capsule wardrobe from these, then add power colours like rust, mustard, olive, teal and burgundy.

Warm Autumn's leading trait is warmth, and its second trait is softness. It sits between Soft Autumn, Deep Autumn and Warm Spring. Compared with Warm Spring, its colours are richer and more muted.

Warm Autumns often have golden, peachy, bronze or freckled skin, auburn, copper, golden brown or warm dark blonde hair, and amber, hazel, warm brown or olive-green eyes.
