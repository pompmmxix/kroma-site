details:
- heading: Best hair colours for Warm Spring
  body: |
    Your natural hair is often golden blonde, strawberry blonde, copper or golden brown. Bright, warm colour suits you best.

    - **Flattering:** golden and honey blonde, strawberry blonde, copper, bright auburn, golden brown and warm caramel highlights.
    - **Harder to wear:** ash blonde, platinum, blue-black and cool, smoky browns. They take the glow out of your skin.
- heading: Jewellery and metals
  body: |
    Polished yellow gold is your metal, along with brass, copper and gold-tone pieces. Shine suits you, because your colouring is clear.

    For stones, choose warm and clear ones: citrine, topaz, amber, coral, turquoise, peridot, carnelian and cream pearls.
- heading: Denim, prints and fabrics
  body: |
    - **Denim:** clear mid-wash blue, plus camel, cream and warm khaki trousers. Grey and black washes look cold on you.
    - **Prints:** cheerful and warm: tropical and garden florals, bright stripes, polka dots and gingham in coral, yellow and green.
    - **Fabrics:** fresh, smooth fabrics: cotton, linen, silk and fine knits. A little sheen is good.
- heading: Colours Warm Spring should avoid
  body: |
    The least flattering colours are cool, dark or dusty: the opposite of warm and clear.

    - **Black** and **optic white**
    - **Icy and cool tones**: icy pink, lavender, blue-grey
    - **Fuchsia**, **magenta** and **blue-reds**
    - **Dusty, greyed colours**, which look dull next to your clear colouring

    Wear them away from your face, or bring coral, warm green or golden yellow up near your face.
- heading: Warm Spring vs Warm Autumn
  body: |
    Both are warm first, so both suit golden colours and gold metals. The difference is clarity and depth.

    | | Warm Spring | Warm Autumn |
    |---|---|---|
    | Feel | Clear, fresh, sunny | Rich, muted, earthy |
    | Best oranges | Tangerine, apricot | Rust, pumpkin, terracotta |
    | Best greens | Grass green, warm turquoise | Olive, moss, deep teal |
    | Best yellows | Golden yellow, buttercup | Mustard, marigold |
    | Neutrals | Light camel, warm ivory, golden tan | Chocolate, camel, olive |

    A quick test: hold bright tangerine and rust under your face in daylight. If tangerine makes you glow and rust looks heavy, you're more likely Warm Spring.
- heading: Warm Spring vs Light Spring and Bright Spring
  body: |
    All three Springs suit warm, clear colours. **Light Spring** is lightest and most delicate: peach, light aqua and buttercup. **Bright Spring** is most vivid and highest-contrast: hot coral, kelly green and bright turquoise. Warm Spring sits between them, with the most golden palette of the three.

  a: >-
    Cream, ivory or warm white. Stark optic white looks cold next to golden skin.
- q: Can Warm Spring wear silver?
  a: >-

Warm Spring's leading trait is warmth, and its second trait is clarity. It sits between Light Spring, Bright Spring and Warm Autumn. Unlike Warm Autumn, its colours are clear and fresh rather than earthy and muted.

Warm Springs often have golden, peachy or freckled skin, with golden blonde, strawberry, copper or warm brown hair and warm green, topaz, hazel or warm blue eyes.
