Personal colour (퍼스널 컬러) is hugely popular in Korean beauty culture. Studios in Seoul drape you in dozens of coloured fabrics under controlled light and tell you your tone. The system starts with one question, warm (웜톤) or cool (쿨톤), then splits each season by depth and clarity:

  • Spring and autumn are always warm; summer and winter are always cool.
  • Light (라이트) and bright (브라이트) types suit clear colours.
  • Mute (뮤트) types suit soft, greyed colours.
  • Deep (딥) types suit dark, rich colours.

Korean personal colour test at home

  1. Sit facing a window in daylight, with no makeup.
  2. Hold gold, then silver, under your chin. Gold that makes your skin glow points to 웜톤 (warm). Silver that makes it look clearer points to 쿨톤 (cool).
  3. Compare a vivid colour with a greyed version of the same colour. If the vivid one looks better, you're light or bright; if the soft one does, you're mute; if dark colours look best, you're deep.
