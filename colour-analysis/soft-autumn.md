details:
- heading: Best hair colours for Soft Autumn
  body: |
    Your natural hair is often dark blonde to medium brown with a soft golden or bronze cast. Colour that stays blended and gently warm suits you best.

    - **Flattering:** golden or honey brown, soft caramel, warm beige blonde, light auburn, and blended, low-contrast balayage.
    - **Harder to wear:** jet black, platinum, ash blonde and bright copper. They're either too high-contrast, too cool or too vivid.
- heading: Jewellery and metals
  body: |
    Soft, warm finishes suit you: brushed or matte gold, antique gold, rose gold and bronze. Very shiny yellow gold can look a little loud; bright silver can look cold.

    For stones and materials, choose muted, earthy ones: amber, peridot, jade, topaz, smoky quartz, citrine, wood, horn and cream pearls.
- heading: Denim, prints and fabrics
  body: |
    - **Denim:** mid-wash and softly faded denim, plus olive, khaki and camel chinos. Very dark indigo and stark white denim add more contrast than you need.
    - **Prints:** blended, earthy and low-contrast: soft paisley, muted florals, heathered tweeds and tonal plaids in olive, camel and teal.
    - **Fabrics:** matte and textured: suede, cashmere, linen, brushed cotton and soft knits.
- heading: Colours Soft Autumn should avoid
  body: |
    The least flattering colours are bright, icy or very cool: the opposite of soft and warm.

    - **Black** and **optic white**, especially together
    - **Neon**, **fuchsia** and **electric blue**
    - **Icy pastels** such as icy pink, icy blue and lavender
    - **Cool greys** and **blue-reds**

    Wear them away from your face, or soften them with a camel, olive or soft teal near your face.
- heading: Soft Autumn vs Soft Summer
  body: |
    These are the most often confused seasons. Both are soft first, so both suit muted colours. The difference is temperature.

    | | Soft Autumn | Soft Summer |
    |---|---|---|
    | Undertone | Warm-neutral | Cool-neutral |
    | Best pinks | Peach, salmon, warm coral | Dusty rose, mauve |
    | Best greens | Olive, moss, khaki | Sage, sea glass, grey-green |
    | Neutrals | Camel, warm taupe, chocolate | Charcoal, cool taupe, soft navy |
    | Metals | Brushed gold, bronze | Brushed silver, pewter |

    A quick test: hold peach and dusty rose under your face in daylight. If peach brings colour to your skin and dusty rose looks greyish on you, you're more likely Soft Autumn.
- heading: Soft Autumn vs Warm Autumn
  body: |
    Warm Autumn (also called True Autumn) is warm first. It suits richer, spicier colours like pumpkin, rust and marigold. Soft Autumn is soft first, so the same colours look better slightly greyed: salmon rather than orange, khaki rather than mustard. If rich, saturated warm colours feel like too much on you, you're more likely Soft Autumn.

Soft Autumn's leading trait is softness, so vivid colours look loud on it. Its second trait is warmth, which separates it from the cooler, greyer Soft Summer.

Soft Autumns often have neutral-warm or olive skin, dark blonde to medium brown hair with a golden or bronze cast, and hazel, olive, soft brown or green eyes.
