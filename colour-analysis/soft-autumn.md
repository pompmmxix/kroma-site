Soft Autumn's leading trait is softness, so vivid colours look loud on it. Its second trait is warmth, which separates it from the cooler, greyer Soft Summer.

Soft Autumns often have neutral-warm or olive skin, dark blonde to medium brown hair with a golden or bronze cast, and hazel, olive, soft brown or green eyes.
