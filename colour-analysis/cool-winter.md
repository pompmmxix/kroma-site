Cool Winter's leading trait is coolness, and its second trait is clarity. It sits between Deep Winter, Bright Winter and Cool Summer. Compared with Cool Summer, its colours are sharper and higher-contrast.

Cool Winters often have cool or neutral-cool skin at any depth, dark brown, black or early silver hair, and dark brown, cool blue, grey or black-brown eyes.
