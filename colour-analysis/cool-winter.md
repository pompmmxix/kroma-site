details:
- heading: Best hair colours for Cool Winter
  body: |
    Your natural hair is often dark and cool: deep brown, black or salt-and-pepper, with no red or gold. Colour that keeps it cool and clear suits you best.

    - **Flattering:** cool dark brown, soft black, blue-black, cool espresso, and silver or white as it comes in. Grey hair often looks striking on Cool Winters.
    - **Harder to wear:** golden brown, copper, caramel highlights and warm honey blonde. They add warmth your colouring doesn't have.
- heading: Jewellery and metals
  body: |
    Cool, bright metals suit you best: polished silver, platinum, white gold and steel. Shine works, because your colouring is clear rather than soft.

    For stones, choose clear, cool and jewel-toned ones: diamonds, crystal, sapphire, ruby, emerald, amethyst, onyx, black pearls and white pearls.
- heading: Denim, prints and fabrics
  body: |
    - **Denim:** dark rinse, black and crisp white denim. Faded, dusty mid-washes can look tired.
    - **Prints:** graphic and high-contrast: black-and-white stripes, houndstooth, polka dots, bold geometrics and clean florals on a black or white base.
    - **Fabrics:** crisp, smooth fabrics: poplin, silk, satin, fine wool, leather and patent. A bit of sheen suits you.
- heading: Colours Cool Winter should avoid
  body: |
    The least flattering colours are warm, muted or golden: the opposite of cool and clear.

    - **Orange**, **rust** and **terracotta**
    - **Camel**, **mustard** and **golden browns**
    - **Peach**, **salmon** and warm corals
    - **Dusty, greyed colours** like sage and mauve, which can look dull on you

    Wear them away from your face if you love them, and keep black, white or a jewel tone near your face.
- heading: Cool Winter vs Cool Summer
  body: |
    Both are cool first, so both suit blue-based colours and silver. The difference is contrast and clarity.

    | | Cool Winter | Cool Summer |
    |---|---|---|
    | Contrast | High | Low to medium |
    | Black | One of your best colours | Too harsh; navy or charcoal instead |
    | Best white | Pure white | Soft white |
    | Best pinks | Fuchsia, magenta | Rose, raspberry |
    | Best blues | Royal, cobalt | Periwinkle, cornflower |

    A quick test: wear pure black and white together, then soft navy and soft white. If black and white looks crisp rather than harsh, you're more likely Cool Winter.
- heading: Cool Winter vs Deep Winter and Bright Winter
  body: |
    All three Winters suit black, white and jewel tones. **Deep Winter** is darkest: it suits deep shades like pine, aubergine and oxblood. **Bright Winter** is most vivid: it suits electric colours like hot pink and electric blue. Cool Winter sits between them, with the iciest, most blue-based palette of the three.
faq:
- q: Is True Winter the same as Cool Winter?
  a: >-
    Yes. Different colour analysis systems use different names for the same season. True Winter, Cool Winter and Pure Winter all describe the coolest Winter type.
- q: Can Cool Winter wear gold?
  a: >-
    Silver, platinum and white gold usually look better. If you like gold, try a cool, pale white gold, or mix it with silver.
- q: Can Cool Winter wear brown?
  a: >-
    Warm browns are among the hardest colours for Cool Winter. If you want a brown, choose a cool, dark one close to charcoal or black-brown, or use cool taupe instead.
- q: Can Cool Winter wear pastels?
  a: >-
    Yes, if they're icy. Icy pink, icy blue, icy lavender and icy mint (pastels with a lot of white in them) suit Cool Winter, especially next to black or navy. Dusty or warm pastels like peach don't.
- q: What are the best neutrals for Cool Winter?
  a: >-
    Black, pure white, charcoal, navy and cool grey. Build a capsule wardrobe from these, then add jewel tones like royal blue, emerald, fuchsia and true red.

Cool Winter's leading trait is coolness, and its second trait is clarity. It sits between Deep Winter, Bright Winter and Cool Summer. Compared with Cool Summer, its colours are sharper and higher-contrast.

Cool Winters often have cool or neutral-cool skin at any depth, dark brown, black or early silver hair, and dark brown, cool blue, grey or black-brown eyes.
