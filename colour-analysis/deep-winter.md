details:
- heading: Best hair colours for Deep Winter
  body: |
    Your natural hair is usually very dark: deep brown, espresso or black. Dark, cool, rich colour suits you best.

    - **Flattering:** black, soft black, cool espresso, deep cool brown, and dark cherry or burgundy tints. Grey and silver often look striking.
    - **Harder to wear:** golden blonde, copper, caramel and light, warm highlights. They're too light or too warm against deep colouring.
- heading: Jewellery and metals
  body: |
    Bright, cool metals suit you best: silver, platinum, white gold and gunmetal. Bold, substantial pieces hold up well against deep colouring.

    For stones, choose deep and jewel-toned ones: ruby, sapphire, emerald, amethyst, garnet, onyx, black pearls and diamonds.
- heading: Denim, prints and fabrics
  body: |
    - **Denim:** dark rinse, deep indigo and black denim, plus crisp white. Light, faded washes look washed out on you.
    - **Prints:** bold and high-contrast: black-and-white graphics, jewel-toned florals on black, animal print in black and white, and strong stripes.
    - **Fabrics:** rich and luxurious: velvet, satin, silk, leather and fine wool. Sheen suits you.
- heading: Colours Deep Winter should avoid
  body: |
    The least flattering colours are light, warm or dusty: the opposite of deep, cool and clear.

    - **Pastels** worn alone, without a dark colour nearby
    - **Orange**, **mustard** and **camel**
    - **Warm browns** like tan and rust
    - **Dusty, greyed colours** like sage and mauve

    If you love these, wear them below the waist, or pair them with black, navy or a jewel tone near your face.
- heading: Deep Winter vs Deep Autumn
  body: |
    These two share the same depth. The difference is temperature, and it shows most in whites, reds and metals.

    | | Deep Winter | Deep Autumn |
    |---|---|---|
    | Undertone | Cool | Warm |
    | Best white | Pure white | Cream, ivory |
    | Best reds | Blue-red, cherry, wine | Brick, tomato, rust-red |
    | Best greens | Emerald, pine | Olive, forest, deep teal |
    | Black | One of your best colours | Wearable; chocolate is softer |
    | Metals | Silver, platinum | Gold, bronze, copper |

    A quick test: hold a blue-red and a brick red under your face in daylight. If the blue-red looks crisp and the brick red looks muddy, you're more likely Deep Winter.
- heading: Deep Winter vs Cool Winter and Bright Winter
  body: |
    All three Winters suit black, white and jewel tones. **Cool Winter** is the iciest, most blue-based of the three. **Bright Winter** is the most vivid, with electric colours like hot pink and electric blue. Deep Winter is the darkest: it suits deep shades like pine, aubergine, oxblood and sapphire best.
faq:
- q: Is Dark Winter the same as Deep Winter?
  a: >-
    Yes. Different colour analysis systems use different names for the same season. Dark Winter and Deep Winter both describe the deepest Winter type.
- q: Can Deep Winter wear brown?
  a: >-
    Yes, if it's very dark and cool, like espresso or black-brown. Tan, camel and golden browns are harder.
- q: Can Deep Winter wear gold?
  a: >-
    Silver, platinum and white gold usually look best. Because Deep Winter borders Deep Autumn, some can wear a deep, rich gold, but bright yellow gold tends to look brassy.
- q: Can Deep Winter wear pastels?
  a: >-
    Icy pastels can work as accents, especially next to black or navy. Worn on their own near the face, they can look washed out on deep colouring.
- q: What are the best neutrals for Deep Winter?
  a: >-
    Black, navy, charcoal, pure white and grey. Build a capsule wardrobe from these, then add power colours like true red, sapphire, emerald, purple and burgundy.

Deep Winter's leading trait is depth, and its second trait is coolness. It's the season that suits true black best. Compared with Deep Autumn, its colours are cooler and clearer.

Deep Winters often have dark brown to black hair, deep brown or black-brown eyes, and skin anywhere from fair to deep with a neutral-cool cast. Many people with East Asian, South Asian and Black heritage are Deep Winters.
