details:
- heading: Best hair colours for Bright Spring
  body: |
    Your natural hair is often medium to dark brown with warm glints, or dark golden blonde, usually with noticeable contrast against your skin and eyes. Rich, clear colour that keeps that contrast suits you best.

    - **Flattering:** warm medium to dark brown, chestnut, bright auburn, golden brown and clean, defined honey or caramel highlights.
    - **Harder to wear:** ash blonde, mousy or smoky browns and heavily blended, muted balayage. They soften the contrast that makes your colouring striking.
- heading: Jewellery and metals
  body: |
    Bright, polished metals suit you: shiny yellow gold, rose gold, and polished silver mixed with gold. Bold, statement pieces work well, because your colouring is vivid.

    For stones, choose clear and saturated ones: coral, turquoise, emerald, citrine, topaz, ruby and clear crystal.
- heading: Denim, prints and fabrics
  body: |
    - **Denim:** clear mid to dark blue washes and crisp cream or white jeans. Faded, dusty washes look tired on you.
    - **Prints:** bold and clear: tropical prints, graphic florals, bright stripes, colour-blocking and geometric patterns.
    - **Fabrics:** smooth, crisp or shiny fabrics: poplin, silk, satin, fine knits and patent leather.
- heading: Colours Bright Spring should avoid
  body: |
    The least flattering colours are dusty, muted or heavy: the opposite of bright and clear.

    - **Dusty, greyed colours** like sage, mauve and dusty rose
    - **Muted earthy colours** like khaki, rust and olive drab
    - **Beige and taupe** near your face, which can look flat
    - **Very dark, heavy colours** worn head to toe

    Pair muted pieces with a clear, saturated colour near your face to bring them to life.
- heading: Bright Spring vs Bright Winter
  body: |
    Both are bright first, so both suit vivid, saturated colours and high contrast. The difference is temperature.

    | | Bright Spring | Bright Winter |
    |---|---|---|
    | Undertone | Warm-neutral | Cool-neutral |
    | Best reds | Tomato, poppy, coral-red | Cherry, true red, blue-red |
    | Best pinks | Hot coral, warm watermelon | Hot pink, fuchsia |
    | Best greens | Kelly green, bright turquoise | Emerald, bright jade |
    | Best white | Bright cream, warm white | Pure white |

    A quick test: hold coral and fuchsia under your face in daylight. If coral brightens your skin and fuchsia looks harsh, you're more likely Bright Spring.
- heading: Bright Spring vs Warm Spring and Light Spring
  body: |
    All three Springs suit warm, clear colours. **Warm Spring** is the most golden: tangerine, marigold and warm green. **Light Spring** is the lightest: peach, light aqua and buttercup. Bright Spring is the most vivid and highest-contrast of the three, and the one that can carry the boldest colours.

Bright Spring's leading trait is clarity, so muted or dusty colours dull it down fast. Its second trait is warmth, which separates it from the cooler, icier Bright Winter.

Bright Springs usually have striking eyes (clear blue, green, turquoise or topaz), noticeable contrast between hair and skin, and hair anywhere from golden blonde to dark brown.
