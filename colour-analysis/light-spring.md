Light Spring sits between Warm Spring and Light Summer. Its leading trait is lightness, so heavy, dark colours tend to overpower it. Its second trait is warmth, which is what separates it from the cooler, rosier Light Summer.

Light Springs usually have low to medium contrast. Hair is often golden or strawberry blonde, light brown, or light auburn, and eyes are commonly light blue, aqua, green or light hazel.
