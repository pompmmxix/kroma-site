details:
- heading: Best hair colours for Light Spring
  body: |
    Your natural hair is often golden blonde, strawberry blonde or light golden brown. Light, warm, clear colour suits you best.

    - **Flattering:** golden, honey and butter blonde, strawberry blonde, light golden brown and fine warm highlights.
    - **Harder to wear:** jet black, dark espresso, ash or platinum blonde and dark auburn. They're too deep, too cool or too heavy for light colouring.
- heading: Jewellery and metals
  body: |
    Light, polished warm metals suit you: yellow gold, rose gold and light gold-tone pieces. Delicate jewellery looks better than chunky, dark or heavily antiqued pieces.

    For stones, choose light, clear and warm ones: citrine, peridot, aquamarine, morganite, light coral, cream pearls and clear crystal.
- heading: Denim, prints and fabrics
  body: |
    - **Denim:** light and mid-wash blue, cream and soft camel jeans. Very dark indigo and black denim look heavy on you.
    - **Prints:** light, fresh and airy: small florals, gingham, light stripes and pastel prints on cream.
    - **Fabrics:** light, smooth fabrics: cotton, linen, chiffon, light knits and silk with a soft sheen.
- heading: Colours Light Spring should avoid
  body: |
    The least flattering colours are dark, heavy or cool: the opposite of light and warm.

    - **Black** and very dark charcoal
    - **Deep jewel tones** like burgundy, aubergine and pine
    - **Dark earthy colours** like rust, chocolate and olive
    - **Cool, greyed colours** like slate and mauve

    Wear darker colours below the waist, and keep a light warm colour like peach, cream or aqua near your face.
- heading: Light Spring vs Light Summer
  body: |
    Both are light first, so both suit pale, delicate colours. The difference is temperature.

    | | Light Spring | Light Summer |
    |---|---|---|
    | Undertone | Warm | Cool |
    | Best pinks | Peach, warm pink, light coral | Powder pink, soft rose |
    | Best blues | Light aqua, warm sky blue | Powder blue, periwinkle |
    | Best white | Cream, warm ivory | Soft white |
    | Metals | Yellow and rose gold | Silver, white gold |

    A quick test: hold peach and powder pink under your face in daylight. If peach makes you look fresh and powder pink looks flat, you're more likely Light Spring.
- heading: Light Spring vs Warm Spring and Bright Spring
  body: |
    All three Springs suit warm, clear colours. **Warm Spring** is the most golden: tangerine, marigold and warm green. **Bright Spring** is the most vivid: hot coral, kelly green and bright turquoise. Light Spring is the lightest and most delicate of the three: peach, light aqua and buttercup yellow.

Light Spring sits between Warm Spring and Light Summer. Its leading trait is lightness, so heavy, dark colours tend to overpower it. Its second trait is warmth, which is what separates it from the cooler, rosier Light Summer.

Light Springs usually have low to medium contrast. Hair is often golden or strawberry blonde, light brown, or light auburn, and eyes are commonly light blue, aqua, green or light hazel.
