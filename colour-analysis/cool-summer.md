details:
- heading: Best hair colours for Cool Summer
  body: |
    Your natural hair is often ash blonde to ash brown, and it may go silver early. Cool, soft colour suits you best.

    - **Flattering:** ash blonde, cool beige blonde, ash or mushroom brown, soft cool lowlights, and silver or grey as it comes in.
    - **Harder to wear:** golden or honey blonde, copper, caramel and jet black. They're too warm or too high-contrast.
- heading: Jewellery and metals
  body: |
    Cool metals suit you: silver, white gold, platinum and pewter. Soft shine or brushed finishes look most natural; very chunky, high-shine pieces can overpower you.

    For stones, choose cool, softly clear ones: pearls, moonstone, aquamarine, blue topaz, rose quartz, amethyst, sapphire and tanzanite.
- heading: Denim, prints and fabrics
  body: |
    - **Denim:** mid-wash and light blue denim, and grey or soft white jeans. Very dark, near-black indigo is a bit severe on you.
    - **Prints:** gentle and medium-contrast: watercolour florals, soft stripes in navy and white, and small prints in rose, blue and grey.
    - **Fabrics:** smooth and fluid: silk, jersey, fine knits, chambray and cotton voile.
- heading: Colours Cool Summer should avoid
  body: |
    The least flattering colours are warm, golden or very stark: the opposite of cool and soft.

    - **Orange**, **rust** and **terracotta**
    - **Mustard**, **camel** and **golden browns**
    - **Black** and **optic white** together, which is too much contrast
    - **Neon** and very saturated brights

    Wear them away from your face, or put a soft blue, rose or grey near your face.
- heading: Cool Summer vs Cool Winter
  body: |
    Both are cool first, so both suit blue-based colours and silver. The difference is contrast and clarity.

    | | Cool Summer | Cool Winter |
    |---|---|---|
    | Contrast | Low to medium | High |
    | Black | Too harsh; navy or charcoal instead | One of the best colours |
    | Best white | Soft white | Pure white |
    | Best pinks | Rose, raspberry | Fuchsia, magenta |
    | Best blues | Periwinkle, cornflower | Royal, cobalt |

    A quick test: try soft navy with soft white, then black with pure white. If navy and soft white look polished while black and white look harsh, you're more likely Cool Summer.
- heading: Cool Summer vs Soft Summer and Light Summer
  body: |
    All three Summers suit cool, gentle colours. **Soft Summer** is the most muted, with smoky colours like sage and slate. **Light Summer** is the lightest, with pastels like powder blue and soft lilac. Cool Summer sits between them: the most blue-based of the three, with medium-depth colours like rose, periwinkle and soft teal.
faq:
- q: Is True Summer the same as Cool Summer?
  a: >-
    Yes. Different colour analysis systems use different names for the same season. True Summer and Cool Summer both describe the coolest Summer type.
- q: Can Cool Summer wear black?
  a: >-
    Black is usually too stark for Cool Summer's softer contrast. Navy, charcoal and slate give the same polished effect. If you love black, wear it below the waist or soften it with grey or rose near your face.
- q: Can Cool Summer wear gold?
  a: >-
    Silver, white gold and platinum usually look better. Rose gold is a good compromise if you like a warmer metal.
- q: Can Cool Summer wear brown?
  a: >-
    Yes, if it's cool: cocoa, cool taupe and rose-brown work well. Golden browns, tan and rust are harder.
- q: What are the best neutrals for Cool Summer?
  a: >-
    Navy, charcoal, grey, soft white and cool cocoa brown. Build a capsule wardrobe from these, then add power colours like soft blue, rose, lavender and soft teal.

Cool Summer's leading trait is coolness, and its second trait is softness. It sits between Light Summer, Soft Summer and Cool Winter. Compared with Cool Winter, its colours are softer and less stark.

Cool Summers often have pink or rosy skin, ash blonde to ash brown hair (often greying early to silver), and grey-blue, blue, grey-green or soft cool hazel eyes.
