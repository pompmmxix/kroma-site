Deep Autumn's leading trait is depth, so light pastels can look washed out next to it. Its second trait is warmth, which separates it from the cooler, icier Deep Winter.

Deep Autumns usually have dark brown to black hair (often with warm or red glints), medium to deep skin with a warm or olive cast, and dark brown, hazel or deep olive eyes. Many people with brown skin are Deep Autumns.
