details:
- heading: Best hair colours for Deep Autumn
  body: |
    Your natural hair is usually dark and warm: deep brown, espresso or near-black with red or golden glints. Colour that keeps the depth and warmth works best.

    - **Flattering:** chocolate, espresso, mahogany, deep auburn, chestnut, and copper or caramel balayage on dark hair.
    - **Harder to wear:** ash blonde, platinum, icy highlights and blue-black. They're too light or too cool, and can make skin look grey.
- heading: Jewellery and metals
  body: |
    Warm metals with some weight suit you: yellow gold, antique or brushed gold, bronze, copper and brass. Bigger, chunkier pieces hold up well against deep colouring.

    For stones and materials, choose rich, warm ones: amber, tiger's eye, garnet, carnelian, smoky quartz, olive jade, wood and tortoiseshell.
- heading: Denim, prints and fabrics
  body: |
    - **Denim:** dark indigo and deep rinse washes, plus olive, chocolate or rust cords. Very pale, bleached denim looks washed out on you.
    - **Prints:** rich, earthy prints: paisley, ikat, leopard and tortoiseshell, autumn florals, and plaids in forest, rust and navy.
    - **Fabrics:** texture suits you. Suede, leather, tweed, velvet, corduroy and chunky knits all echo your depth.
- heading: Colours Deep Autumn should avoid
  body: |
    The least flattering colours are light, icy or cool: the opposite of deep and warm.

    - **Pastels** such as baby pink, powder blue and mint
    - **Icy and cool tones**: icy grey, lavender, cool fuchsia
    - **Stark optic white**, which looks harsh next to warm, deep colouring
    - **Dusty, greyed colours**, which can look muddy

    If you love any of these, wear them away from your face, or anchor them with a deep warm colour near your face.
- heading: Deep Autumn vs Deep Winter
  body: |
    These two share the same depth. The difference is temperature, and it shows most in whites, reds and metals.

    | | Deep Autumn | Deep Winter |
    |---|---|---|
    | Undertone | Warm | Cool |
    | Best white | Cream, ivory | Pure white |
    | Best reds | Brick, tomato, rust-red | Blue-red, cherry, wine |
    | Best greens | Olive, forest, deep teal | Emerald, pine |
    | Black | Wearable; chocolate is softer | One of your best colours |
    | Metals | Gold, bronze, copper | Silver, platinum |

    A quick test: hold a brick red and a blue-red under your face in daylight. If brick red makes your skin glow and blue-red looks harsh, you're more likely Deep Autumn.
- heading: Deep Autumn vs Warm Autumn
  body: |
    Warm Autumn (also called True Autumn) is warm first and medium-deep. It suits golden, glowing colours like pumpkin and marigold. Deep Autumn is deep first, so it needs darker shades: espresso rather than camel, burgundy rather than terracotta. If dark colours look natural on you rather than heavy, you're more likely Deep Autumn.
faq:
- q: Can Deep Autumn wear black?
  a: >-
    Yes. Deep Autumn is deep enough to wear black, especially in outfits with plenty of contrast. Dark chocolate, espresso, deep olive and navy often look even richer, so try them where you'd normally reach for black.
- q: Can Deep Autumn wear silver?
  a: >-
    Gold, bronze and copper usually look better. If you like silver, choose oxidised or antiqued silver, or mix it with gold so it doesn't look cold against your skin.
- q: What pinks suit Deep Autumn?
  a: >-
    Warm, deep pinks: terracotta pink, deep coral, rose-brown and warm raspberry. Baby pink and icy pink are the hardest.
- q: Is Deep Autumn warm or cool?
  a: >-
    Warm, leaning neutral. It's the coolest of the Autumn seasons because it borders Deep Winter, but its best colours still have a golden base.
- q: What are the best neutrals for Deep Autumn?
  a: >-
    Navy, charcoal, chocolate, espresso, camel, olive, cream and beige. Build a capsule wardrobe from these, then add power colours like burgundy, teal, olive and mustard.

Deep Autumn's leading trait is depth, so light pastels can look washed out next to it. Its second trait is warmth, which separates it from the cooler, icier Deep Winter.

Deep Autumns usually have dark brown to black hair (often with warm or red glints), medium to deep skin with a warm or olive cast, and dark brown, hazel or deep olive eyes. Many people with brown skin are Deep Autumns.
