details:
- heading: Best hair colours for Soft Summer
  body: |
    Your natural hair is often the best guide: ash brown, mushroom brown or dark ash blonde, with no obvious gold. If you colour it, stay in that family.

    - **Flattering:** ash or mushroom brown, cool beige blonde, soft ash blonde, gentle cool lowlights, and grey or silver as it comes in.
    - **Harder to wear:** jet black, bright copper, brassy golden blonde and very high-contrast highlights. They add contrast and warmth your colouring doesn't have.
- heading: Jewellery and metals
  body: |
    Soft, low-shine finishes suit you best. Brushed or matte silver, pewter, white gold and soft rose gold all sit well against cool-neutral skin. Polished yellow gold tends to look brassy.

    For stones and beads, look for the same dusty quality as your clothes: pearls, moonstone, rose quartz, amethyst, aquamarine, grey or smoky quartz and jade.
- heading: Denim, prints and patterns
  body: |
    - **Denim:** mid-wash, grey-blue and softly faded denim. Very dark, near-black indigo and bright acid wash are both too high-contrast.
    - **Prints:** blended, watercolour-like and low-contrast. Florals in rose, sage and slate, soft heathered knits and tonal stripes work. Bold graphic black-and-white prints fight your colouring.
    - **Fabrics:** matte and textured fabrics like cashmere, linen, chambray and brushed cotton flatter more than high-shine satin in bright colours.
- heading: Colours Soft Summer should avoid
  body: |
    The colours that look least good are the opposite of your traits: bright, warm or very high-contrast.

    - **Black** and **optic white**, especially together
    - **Orange**, **bright yellow** and **mustard**, which are warm and saturated
    - **Neon** and **pure primaries**: fire-engine red, cobalt, emerald
    - **Golden camel** and **rust**, which suit the warmer Soft Autumn better

    You don't need to throw these out. Wear them away from your face (trousers, shoes, bags), or soften them with a Soft Summer colour near your face.
- heading: Soft Summer vs Soft Autumn
  body: |
    These two are the most often confused seasons. Both are soft first, so both suit muted, greyed colours. The difference is temperature.

    | | Soft Summer | Soft Autumn |
    |---|---|---|
    | Undertone | Cool-neutral | Warm-neutral |
    | Best pinks | Dusty rose, mauve | Peach, salmon, warm coral |
    | Best greens | Sage, sea glass, grey-green | Olive, moss, khaki |
    | Neutrals | Charcoal, cool taupe, soft navy | Camel, warm taupe, chocolate |
    | Metals | Brushed silver, pewter | Brushed gold, bronze |

    A quick test: hold dusty rose and peach under your face in daylight. If dusty rose evens out your skin and peach makes it look sallow, you're more likely Soft Summer.
- heading: Soft Summer vs Cool Summer
  body: |
    Cool Summer (also called True Summer) is cool first and soft second. It can handle clearer colours: raspberry, true blue, cool emerald. On a Soft Summer those look slightly too sharp. If your best colours look greyed and smoky rather than clear and cool, you're probably Soft Summer.
faq:
- q: Can Soft Summer wear black?
  a: It's one of the hardest colours for Soft Summer, because it adds more contrast and depth than your colouring has. Charcoal, soft navy or deep slate give the same polished effect. If you love black, wear it below the waist or break it up with a soft colour near your face.
- q: What white should Soft Summer wear?
  a: A soft white or a cool, slightly grey cream. Stark optic white looks harsh, and yellowish ivory can look warm and dull next to your skin.
- q: Is Soft Summer warm or cool?
  a: Cool-neutral. It's the warmest of the three Summer seasons, which is why it borders Soft Autumn, but its best colours still lean cool.
- q: Can Soft Summer wear brown?
  a: Yes, if it's cool and greyed. Cocoa, mushroom, taupe and greige all work. Golden, reddish browns like rust and tan are harder to wear.
- q: What are the best neutrals for Soft Summer?
  a: Charcoal, soft navy, grey, cool taupe, greige and soft white. Build the base of a capsule wardrobe from these, then add power colours like dusty rose, sage and soft teal.

Soft Summer's leading trait is softness, also called low chroma. Bright, saturated colours look loud next to it, while muted, greyed colours make skin look smooth and even. Its second trait is coolness, which separates it from the warmer Soft Autumn.

Soft Summers often have neutral-cool skin, ash or mousy brown hair, and grey-green, grey-blue or soft hazel eyes. Hair, skin and eyes tend to blend rather than stand apart.
