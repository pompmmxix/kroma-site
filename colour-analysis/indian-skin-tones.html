A lot of colour advice was written with fair skin in mind, so brown skin ends up with one-line answers like "wear jewel tones" or "avoid beige". The real answer is more useful: work out your undertone (warm, cool or neutral), your depth (light, medium or deep) and your chroma (bright and clear, or soft and blended). Those three traits decide your colours, whatever your heritage.

"Dusky" is a common search term in India for medium-deep and deep skin. We use medium-deep and deep here.

Step 1: Find your undertone

Undertone is the colour under the surface of your skin, and it doesn't change when you tan. On brown skin it's often easier to see in jewellery than in veins.

  • Warm (golden, yellow, olive-gold): gold jewellery glows, cream looks better than white, and your skin can look grey or ashy in icy colours.
  • Cool (pink, red or blue-red): silver looks crisp, pure white looks fresh, and orange or mustard can make you look tired.
  • Neutral: both metals work. Many South Asian skin tones are neutral or olive-neutral. Let depth and chroma do more of the work.

Colours that work on most brown skin

  • Jewel tones (emerald, sapphire, ruby, amethyst) suit most medium and deep skin because they have the depth to match it. Warm skin leans to emerald and ruby; cool skin to sapphire and amethyst.
  • White and cream: crisp white suits cool and high-contrast colouring; cream and ivory suit warm. Both look great against deep skin.
  • Beige and nude: these work when they're a few shades lighter or darker than your skin, not an exact match. Camel and caramel are good warm alternatives.
  • Pastels: choose warm, slightly saturated pastels (peach, mint, butter yellow) on warm skin, and clear cool ones (powder blue, lilac) on cool skin. Greyed pastels are the hardest to wear.

The Ritu palettes

Kroma also offers Ritu, a colour lens drawn from the six seasons (ritus) of the Indian calendar and the colour worlds of Indian textiles and festivals. Classifying a person by ritu is Kroma's own interpretation, not an established tradition, but many people find it speaks to the colours they already wear for festivals and weddings.

Makeup for brown skin

The same traits apply. Warm undertones suit brick, terracotta, warm berry and caramel lips; cool undertones suit plum, wine, cool berry and blue-red. Choose a foundation by undertone first and depth second, and test it on your jawline in daylight.
