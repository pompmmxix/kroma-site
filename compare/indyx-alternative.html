Choose Indyx if you want…

  • Real human stylists who build outfits from your closet (paid)
  • A peer-to-peer resale marketplace with 0% commission
  • In select US cities, a paid service will catalogue your wardrobe for you at home

Choose Kroma if you want…

  • Kroma doesn't need an account and keeps your wardrobe on your phone
  • Kroma includes colour and body type analysis, and scores wishlist items against them before you buy
  • Kroma's outfit suggestions learn from what you actually wear and are included free; Indyx's styling is done by people, and is paid
