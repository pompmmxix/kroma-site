Choose Acloset if you want…

  • Imports past orders from shops and order emails, so adding clothes is quick
  • Virtual try-on with an AI avatar, plus a community feed and challenges
  • 18 languages, and a large community (8M+ users, by Acloset's own figures)

Choose kroma if you want…

  • kroma has no item limit and no subscription; Acloset's free plan stops at 100 items
  • kroma has no ads or tracking and keeps your wardrobe on your phone; Acloset stores it on its servers and shows ads
  • kroma's colour analysis offers five systems, including Korean Tonal, Ritu and Shikisai
