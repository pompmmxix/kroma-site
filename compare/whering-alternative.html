Choose Whering if you want…

  • A huge database of items to add from, plus a web app and Chrome extension, so building your wardrobe is fast
  • Social features: view and style friends' wardrobes
  • Available on Android, iOS, Mac and the web today

Choose kroma if you want…

  • kroma has no account and keeps your wardrobe on your phone; Whering is account-based and stores it in the cloud
  • kroma has no analytics or tracking; Whering's privacy policy lists several analytics tools
  • kroma includes personal colour analysis in five systems and body type analysis; Whering doesn't analyse your colouring or shape
