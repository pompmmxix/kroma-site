The short answer

  • Pick Whering for the fastest way to build your wardrobe (a large item database, web app and Chrome extension), weather-aware planning, and social features, all free.
  • Pick Indyx if you want real human stylists, a commission-free resale marketplace, or someone to catalogue your wardrobe for you, and you're happy to pay for those.
  • Consider Kroma if you want colour and body type analysis built in, no account, and your wardrobe kept on your phone.
