Choose Stylebook if you want…

  • A one-off price with no subscription, from a small independent team
  • Private by design too: no account, and your closet data isn't sent to the company
  • Over 15 years of refinement, with a free-form outfit canvas and detailed style stats

Choose Kroma if you want…

  • Kroma runs on Android (launching shortly), which Stylebook doesn't support
  • Kroma adds colour and body type analysis and scores your clothes and wishlist against them
  • Kroma's AI tags garments from a photo and suggests outfits from what you actually wear
