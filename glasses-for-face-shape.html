How to find your face shape

Pull your hair back, look straight into a mirror, and compare four measurements: forehead width, cheekbone width, jaw width and face length.

  • Oval: longer than wide, with a forehead slightly wider than a gently rounded jaw.
  • Round: about as wide as long, with full cheeks and a soft jaw.
  • Square: about as wide as long, with a strong, angular jaw and a wide forehead.
  • Heart: a wide forehead and cheekbones narrowing to a pointed chin.
  • Oblong or rectangle: clearly longer than wide, with straight sides.
  • Diamond: cheekbones are the widest point, with a narrower forehead and jaw.
  • Triangle: the jaw is the widest point and the forehead is narrower.

Common questions

Can I find my face shape from a photo?

You can, but camera angle and hair throw it off easily. A straight-on mirror check with your hair back is more reliable. Kroma asks you to choose your face shape with a guided comparison for the same reason.

How wide should my glasses be?

The frame should be about as wide as your face at the temples, with your eyes roughly centred in each lens. The top of the frame should follow your brow line.
